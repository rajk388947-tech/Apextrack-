# ApexTrack 🏋️

**Strength & Workout Progress Platform**

ApexTrack is a fitness-focused web project centered on strength training and workout progress. It aims to give people a clear place to follow their training journey, stay consistent, and work toward measurable fitness goals.

🌐 **Live website:** https://apextrack-strength-workout-progress-platform.ai.studio/

## Overview

The goal of ApexTrack is to make strength-training progress easier to understand and manage. Whether you're starting a new routine or building on an established one, the platform is designed around the idea that consistent tracking helps inform better training decisions.

## Project Goals

- Make workout progress easier to follow over time.
- Encourage consistency in strength training.
- Help users focus on measurable fitness goals.
- Provide an approachable, fitness-oriented web experience.

## Who It's For

- Beginners who want to build a consistent workout habit.
- Strength-training enthusiasts monitoring their progress.
- Fitness-focused users looking for a dedicated training platform.
